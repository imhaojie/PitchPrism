# Demonstration video

The current video is distributed as [v1.1.0 / enhanced.mp4](https://github.com/imhaojie/PitchPrism/releases/download/v1.1.0/enhanced.mp4): **118.4 seconds**, **1920×1080**, **25fps**, English burned-in text. It opens with a branded cold open and includes three 13.2-second tactical freezes with nine readings. Each freeze moves from the broadcast frame into a 3D tactical view and shows option arrows, covered lanes, carry arrows and space/pressure zones measured from the pitch markings. A half-speed replay and a full-time card follow. No generated narration is included.

This is an enhanced match excerpt. It does not itself demonstrate a successful Azure inference call. The public web demo adds interactive chapters, a Tactical Studio, viewing preferences and evidence inspection. [Detailed media timing and reproducibility](MEDIA.md).

The earlier synthetic screen recording and earlier match exports are historical previews. They are not the current video or its validation evidence. Competition video-platform and eligibility requirements are tracked separately in [submission notes](SUBMISSION.md).
